## 📊 Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard is designed to analyze supply chain operations, monitor business performance, evaluate shipping efficiency, and identify trends in product profitability and regional sales.

By transforming raw business data into meaningful visualizations, the project demonstrates how Business Intelligence tools can support operational monitoring, performance evaluation, and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall supply chain and logistics performance.
- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Evaluate shipping and delivery efficiency.
- Identify profitable products and analyze revenue trends.
- Examine regional and customer-level performance.
- Provide actionable insights to support business decisions.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a consolidated view of business performance through essential KPIs and interactive visualizations.

### 2. Delivery & Shipping Analysis
Focuses on analyzing logistics performance, shipping trends, and delivery efficiency to identify potential operational improvements.

### 3. Product & Profitability Analysis
Evaluates product performance, revenue distribution, and profitability to support strategic business planning.

### 4. Regional & Customer Analysis
Explores geographical performance variations and customer contributions to better understand business trends.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, preparation, and transformation.
- **DAX:** Development of calculated measures and business KPIs.
- **Data Modeling:** Building relationships between datasets for effective analysis.
- **Business Intelligence:** Translating business data into meaningful insights.

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaning and transforming raw datasets using Power Query.
2. **Data Modeling:** Organizing tables and establishing relationships.
3. **DAX Calculations:** Creating calculated measures to evaluate business performance.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Designing interactive reports with charts, filters, and KPIs.
5. **Business Analysis:** Exploring performance trends and identifying areas for improvement.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard demonstrates how data visualization and analytical techniques can help organizations:

- Improve visibility into supply chain operations.
- Monitor shipping and delivery performance.
- Understand product profitability and business trends.
- Evaluate customer and regional contributions.
- Support informed, data-driven strategic decisions.
